why we're here

A cup should feel like a small pause.

Tasa means "cup" in Filipino. Owner Eiron Van T. Yano started it with one grinder, four tables, and a simple idea: good coffee shouldn't be rushed, or overpriced.

Our beans come from highland farms in Bukidnon and Sultan Kudarat. We pay farmers fairly, roast weekly, and brew every cup fresh.
